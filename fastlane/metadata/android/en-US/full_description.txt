Calypso Gallery is an independently maintained, unofficial personal fork of Fossify Gallery (https://github.com/FossifyOrg/Gallery). It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Fossify.

Unleash memories, not personal data. Calypso Gallery is a private photo and video app with a built-in editor, no ads, no Internet permission, and no unnecessary permissions.

🖼️ PHOTO EDITING AT YOUR FINGERTIPS:
Enhance your photos with the built-in editor. Crop, resize, rotate, flip, draw, and apply filters without compromising your privacy.

🌐 PRIVACY FIRST, ALWAYS:
The app does not request Internet access. Strip EXIF metadata such as GPS coordinates and camera details to keep your memories yours.

🔐 CRYPTOGRAPHIC SECRET VAULT:
Protect selected media with a zero-network cryptographic vault. Files are encrypted on disk, metadata is protected, keys are derived with modern key-derivation functions and wrapped by Android Keystore, and the vault supports additional screen and memory protections.

🔗 ZERO-FOOTPRINT VIRTUAL ALBUMS:
Organize media into Virtual Albums without duplicating files. Albums created from encrypted vault items remain hidden outside the unlocked vault.

🔄 RECOVER WITH EASE:
Recover deleted photos and videos with the built-in recycle bin.

🎨 YOUR GALLERY, YOUR STYLE:
Customize themes, colors, and behavior to match your preferences.

📷 UNIVERSAL FORMAT FREEDOM:
JPEG, JPEG XL, PNG, MP4, MKV, RAW, SVG, GIF, AVIF, videos, and more.

✨ MATERIAL DESIGN WITH DYNAMIC THEMES:
Use the Material Design interface with dynamic color and theme support.
